# Superseded records, kept

Records and files moved here are kept as the audit trail of an error, outside every reader: `scripts/check_replay.py`
walks `store/runs/` only, the ledgers read `store/runs/` only, and the guest trace store is `store/traces/`.

## case-runner-1792916257296645000 and case-runner-traces/ (2026-10-25, Sprint 154)

Sprint 150's record of the case runner (commit c1c8ee44f) and its 22 per-run verdict files. The record's
EXECUTION_COMPLETED and TRACE_INGESTED events treat the verdict files as guest traces, and TRACE_INGESTED states that
every line was validated against the vocabulary; no line was a guest line and nothing validated them. They were written
to meet rule T4. reviewer-2's review of Sprints 149-150 found it (High 1). Sprint 152 removed both with `git rm`, which
the kit forbids; Sprint 154 restored them from c1c8ee44f and moved them here with `git mv`.

They are superseded by Sprint 152's eight sessions (`store/runs/case-runner-17929201*` and
`store/runs/case-runner-rust-17929202*`) and Sprint 153's four (`store/runs/case-runner-rust-1792922*`), which carry no
execution or trace event, as `src/w2m/differential.py`'s records carried none.

## runtime-symbols-zero-halfword-2026-10-28.tsv (2026-10-28, Sprint 188)

The runtime image's symbols with a zero halfword below bit 48, which `crates/translator/tests/c_words.rs` held from
Sprint 179 (the record's header: `w2m_approximate` at 0x7f0004060000) and from Sprint 184 (the image the build makes:
`a64_movz` at 0x7f0004170000). The list held nothing: `a64_mov_imm` emits halfword 0 always and skips only a zero
halfword 1, 2 or 3, and every runtime image address has halfwords 1 and 2 nonzero, so no listed symbol was ever at risk
(reviewer-2's Medium 1 of its review of Sprints 182-184). It is superseded by the compile-time assertion under
`RUNTIME_IMAGE_VA` in `crates/guest_runtime/src/layout.rs` (Decision "Sequence after Sprint 185").
