## Installs left out of the burst (reviewer-3's Low 1 at 266)

The gate counts compiles and not installs. An install's time, by subtraction on Sprint 263's adjacent timed pairs
(`compile_critical_path_ms` "vm", the cached run's 125,515 compiles taken at the uncached run's mean):

| Uncached record | ms | a compile | Cached record | ms | its compiles | 145,570 installs | an install |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `w2m-vm-game-1793419013000000000` | 1,296.41 | 4.717 µs | `w2m-vm-game-1793419040000000000` | 693.66 | 592.06 ms | 101.6 ms | 0.70 µs |
| `w2m-vm-game-1793419066000000000` | 1,295.08 | 4.712 µs | `w2m-vm-game-1793419092000000000` | 684.62 | 591.45 ms | 93.2 ms | 0.64 µs |
| `w2m-vm-game-1793419118000000000` | 1,284.64 | 4.674 µs | `w2m-vm-game-1793419145000000000` | 678.18 | 586.69 ms | 91.5 ms | 0.63 µs |

An install takes about a seventh of a compile. Weighted so, and with the cached run's most installs put in the same
frame as its most compiles, which the records do not show, the cached frame's work is 19,201 compiles (89.7 to 90.6
ms) and 33,363 installs (21.0 to 23.3 ms), 111 to 114 ms, against 54,996 compiles, 257 to 259 ms, without the cache:
56% less. Leaving installs out of the gate does not make it pass.
