# signals/0.14 — rationale

Successor to `signals/0.13.json`, locked 2026-11-26. 0.1 to 0.13 stay on disk unchanged.

## What changed

| Proposal | Kind | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| P30 | ENUM_EXTENDED | `metric` gains `lock_hold_ns_p50`, `lock_hold_ns_p99`, `lock_wait_ns_p50`, `lock_wait_ns_p99`, `lock_takings`, `runtime_word_takings` | Change 1 has built four leaf locks (the heap, Sprint 343; the TLS and FLS slots, 345; the handle table, 355; the sync objects, 356), G6 asks for each lock's hold and wait on the record, and 0.13 names the runtime word's alone; a record counted the word's takings only inside `variant` (Sprint 356's Rubber Duck Pass, observation 2). |
| P31 | PAYLOAD_FIELD_PROPOSED | MEASUREMENT_RECORDED gains the optional `lock`, a new enum `lock`: `heap`, `slots`, `handles`, `sync_objects` | A `lock_*` figure measures one leaf lock, and a reader dispatches on which; the values are the service table's leaf `Lock` classes (`crates/guest_runtime/src/services.rs`), held equal to them by a test. |
| P32 | INVARIANT_PROPOSED | E16: MEASUREMENT_RECORDED's `lock` present exactly on the five `lock_*` metrics | The type alone lets `lock` ride on any metric, and leaves a `lock_*` metric free to name none. |

Nothing else changes: `git diff --no-index --stat signals/0.13.json signals/0.14.json` gives "1 file changed, 30
insertions(+), 9 deletions(-)": the version fields, `metric`'s six values, the enum `lock`, MEASUREMENT_RECORDED's
optional `lock`, its type and the note's new sentence, and E16. The tag count stays 62, 38 live and 24 deprecated; the
enums go from 35 to 36. `guest_plane_compatible_versions` is unchanged, since no guest tag changes.

## What each word means

- **`lock_hold_ns_p50`** and **`lock_hold_ns_p99`**: the median and 99th percentile of a leaf lock's hold, from its
  take to its release, unit `ns`. **`lock_wait_ns_p50`** and **`lock_wait_ns_p99`**: the same of the wait to take it.
  They are written as `runtime_word_hold_ns_*` and `runtime_word_wait_ns_*` are. `unit` is `text`, and the vocabulary
  ties no metric to a unit.
- **`lock_takings`**: the times a leaf lock was taken, unit `count`, as `wfe_exits` and `locked_model_steps` are
  written. **`runtime_word_takings`**: the times the runtime word was taken, unit `count`, a figure where 0.13's records
  held it in `variant` only (Sprint 356: 1,576,566 and 69,264 on `semaphore_units` on two vCPUs).
- **`lock`**: the leaf lock a `lock_*` metric measures: `heap` (the process heap's, `heap.c` `hold`), `slots` (the TLS
  and FLS slots', `thread_storage.c` `hold_slots`), `handles` (the handle table's, `sync_objects.c` `hold_handles`),
  `sync_objects` (the sync objects', `sync_objects.c` `hold_sync`). Each is `Lock`'s variant of that name in snake case.
  `Lock::RuntimeWord` is not a value: the word keeps its own metrics, `runtime_word_*`, split by `holder` (P27, E15).
  `Lock::None` is no lock. The Decision "The vocabulary for each lock's hold and wait" (2026-11-25) named `runtime_word`
  among the values; card 357 leaves it out for those reasons, and a `lock` of `runtime_word` is refused (its first
  plant). `services::tests::the_vocabularys_lock_values_are_the_leaf_classes_of_lock` reads `Lock`'s variants from
  `services.rs` and the enum from the vocabulary `signals/rust-current.json` names, and fails until a class Change 1
  adds is named here.
- **E16** (P32): `lock` is present, not null, exactly when `metric` is one of the five `lock_*` values. Both directions
  hold: a `lock_*` figure with no lock names no lock it measures. `crates/w2m_vm/src/bin/check_records.rs` checks it on
  every MEASUREMENT_RECORDED that gives a `lock` or a `lock_*` metric, naming "lock L on metric M" or "metric M with no
  lock". The check takes E16's metrics, and now E15's, as the values of `enums.metric` the constraint's spec names:
  `runtime_word_takings` carries the prefix `runtime_word_`, which the check and the samples used for E15 in 0.13, and
  is not one of E15's four metrics, so the prefix no longer decides.

## Who writes them

No code emits any of the new words; card 358, the per-lock measurement, does. The one writer of MEASUREMENT_RECORDED
today (`crates/w2m_vm/src/record.rs` `measurement`, for the batch run and `run_measurements`) sets `lock` to none: its
lines measure the run and the runtime word. The samples run (`harness/vocabulary-samples.toml`) writes each new
`metric` value once in its every-metric loop and each `lock` value once on `lock_hold_ns_p50`, through their Rust types.

## The two pointers

`signals/rust-current.json` names 0.14. `signals/current.json` keeps naming 0.9, the frozen Python host's.
