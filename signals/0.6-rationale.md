# signals/0.6 — rationale

Successor to `signals/0.5.json`, locked 2026-10-09. 0.1-0.5 stay on disk unchanged.

## What changed

| Proposal | Kind | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| P15 | TAG_PROPOSED | harness tag `SERVICE_UNIMPLEMENTED` (execution_id, service, guest_address, instructions_executed); it closes an execution | The frontier (roadmap Sprint 038) stops when the game calls a Windows service w2m lacks. No tag fit: `EXECUTION_FAILED` is the harness failing to obtain an execution, `EXECUTION_COMPLETED` needs a trace and an exit status, and `BRIDGE_CALL_UNMAPPED` is a Steam interface method with no Mac mapping. The roadmap and review 2026-10-09 (progress) had planned to use `BRIDGE_CALL_UNMAPPED`; reading its definition showed the misuse. |
| P16 | ENUM_EXTENDED | `acquisition_tool` gains `steamcmd` | Both depots were fetched with Valve's SteamCMD (Decision 2026-10-09); the enum could not say so. |

## Compatibility

Guest tags are unchanged, so 0.6 ingests traces written under 0.1-0.6. The harness plane gains one tag and one enum value; records written under 0.5 stay valid.
