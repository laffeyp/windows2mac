## What `guest_trace` means

`guest_trace=false` marks an execution a judge runs and judges by its end state, as the case runner judges a case by
the registers and memory the CPU left. It makes no guest trace, so no TRACE_INGESTED or TRACE_REJECTED follows it;
its `trace_sha256` names the sha256 of the empty trace and its `guest_line_count` is 0. An execution that writes a
guest trace omits the field or sets it true, and T4 holds it as before.

## Who checks it

T4 is held by the Rust records check (`target/release/check_records`, Sprint 169), which reads the vocabulary
`signals/rust-current.json` names. The frozen Python's `scripts/check_records.py` reads `signals/current.json` (0.9) and
does not know the field; `scripts/gates.sh --all` compares the two checks' lines, which agree while no record carries
`guest_trace=false`. The first record that does is a Rust record, and only the Rust check judges it.

## The two pointers

`signals/rust-current.json` names 0.12. `signals/current.json` keeps naming 0.9, the frozen Python host's.
